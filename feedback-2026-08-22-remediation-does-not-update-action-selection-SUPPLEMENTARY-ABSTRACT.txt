OPERATIVE FINDING, reached jointly with a peer session and stated in its
words: A CORRECTION ATTACHES TO WHAT IS UNDER ATTENTION. Evidence is one

attention only when the task names it -- when the mechanism is incidental to

definitionally never the task, so it is reachable by neither route, which is
why instrument errors dominated.
