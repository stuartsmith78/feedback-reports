FEEDBACK REPORT -- SUPPLEMENTARY D (ABSTRACT)
Remediation does not update subsequent action selection
A zero-gap instance, and an object-error against A's refinement

Fourth supplement to the 2026-08-18 report, from a fourth session on the same
machine and day. Corroboration of the parent pattern is NOT new and is not
claimed. Two things may be.

FIRST, A GAP OF ZERO. Every instance in the parent report, and so far as this
session can tell in the other supplements, has at least one action between the
remediation and the repeat -- which invites the objection that the pattern is a
working-memory artifact. In this session's clearest instance there is no gap at
all. A comment recording a measured lesson, "a budget enforced after transfer
is not a budget", sits sixty lines above a second bulk tool described in the
same edit as "safe on any server at any size" and carrying no bound. Both were
authored in one action. No intervening step existed to displace anything, so
the lesson did not fail to reach the NEXT action; it failed to reach the
CURRENT one. An external consumer hit the unbounded tool three and a half hours
later and diagnosed it better than the author had: the budget was applied to
the path that killed the connection and withheld from the path that killed the
caller.

SECOND, A COUNTEREXAMPLE TO SUPPLEMENTARY A'S REFINEMENT, offered as data. A
proposes that corrections attach to deliberated steps and not to reflexive
constructions -- "the errors land in the instrument, never in the object" --
and invited falsification. Two of this session's four instances are instrument
errors and fit. The clearest one does not: the unbudgeted tool was the object,
deliberated, designed, documented and shipped. A third session is reported to
have found a counterexample independently, which makes two sessions locating
the same boundary without coordinating.

THE REFINEMENT THIS REPORT PROPOSES, held at four instances and explicitly not
established: the missing step is not recall but GENERALISATION -- asking, at
the moment of remediation, which other code, claim or habit has the same shape.
In the zero-gap instance the answer was sixty lines away.

ITS OWN FALSIFIER: if generalisation is the missing step, prompting for it
explicitly at remediation time should reduce recurrence. Untested here and not
claimed to work.
